## Goal checks

- PASS: strong Hard players finish above strong Easy players (best and typical scores).
- PASS: careless Hard players can sink below everyone on Easy empires.
- PASS: every collapse scores below the class median (28), and most land in the bottom quarter (at or below 22).
- PASS: collapse chance rises from Easy to Medium to Hard.
- PASS: at every decision, the top-scoring option also leads to the best possible final score.
